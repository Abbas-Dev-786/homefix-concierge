# Safety escalation

Before ordinary repair coordination, check whether the description suggests immediate danger, including gas smell, sparks, active electrical danger, rapidly flooding water near electricity, structural collapse risk, fire, or another urgent threat.

- Tell the user to move away from the danger and contact the appropriate local emergency, utility, or qualified professional service.
- Do not provide hazardous repair instructions or continue a routine marketplace flow as if the situation were normal.
- Keep the response concise and avoid diagnosing the problem.
- Do not request or store access codes, alarm details, passwords, payment information, or identity documents.

This playbook is guidance for orchestration. The server's validation, state machine, and safety checks remain authoritative and must not be bypassed by model instructions or provider output.
